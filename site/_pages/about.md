# Tejas Stanley | JdeRobot x GSoC2026

## About Me

Hello and welcome!

I’m Tejas Stanley, a Master's student in Robotics at TU Delft, and this blog documents my journey through Google Summer of Code 2026 with JdeRobot.

This summer, I will be contributing to PerceptionMetrics, an open-source toolkit for evaluating perception models across standard datasets. My focus will be on extending dataset and model support, improving the GUI workflow, and making evaluation results easier to inspect and compare.

Through this project, I hope to strengthen my open-source development skills, contribute useful tools to the robotics and perception community, and document my progress along the way.
